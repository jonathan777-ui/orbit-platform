Single-chain execution

All project managers, stakeholders, suppliers, and channel partners operate inside one shared thread. The result is less friction, fewer duplicate records, and clearer accountability.

  • Account holder verification before final switch authorization
  • Channel locks that block duplicate submissions and competitor back-channeling
  • Automated settlement and notices dispatched to every party in the chain

Protected market access

Channel protection is enforced as a business rule, not a courtesy. If a competitor tries to circumvent the relationship, the platform triggers a formal LOE and revenue reallocation workflow.

  • Originating partner retains trailing share while new servicing partner takes active share
  • Utility freeze and competitor LOE enforcement active at execution time
  • Customer response cycles trigger only when net loss is created
